# R19-cal-shape: the Calendar's days are not taller than wide, nothing blank under it, the placeholder inside the box

**Paul, 2026-10-12:** *"The calendar is oddly vertically stretched and there's no padding in the 'what' box."* Measured on main (71de1495) on the dev server, the Calendar's blank document: at 390 by 844 in WebKit a day cell is 56 wide by 83 tall (`.cal-cell` `min-height`, a rem of 20.8px under the base size), and at 1100 wide 151 by 120; under the grid and the month's list the column has 385px (phone) and 327px (desktop) of blank, `.to-main.to-wide`'s `padding-bottom: calc(var(--to-col-h) / 2)` (r18-caret-middle: room so a caret typed at the end of a column of words stays at the middle). The "What" box in the event dialog (`.cal-what to-text .tx`, `src/apps/cal/style.css`) has 13px of padding and a border; but its placeholder, "Event", is drawn by the kit at the box's very corner: `to-text .tx.blank::before` in `src/styles/text.css` is `position: absolute; inset: 0 auto auto 0`, which ignores the box's padding. Three fixes, each one rule and one test.

## The design

- **A day is at most as tall as it is wide, until its events need more.** `.cal-cell`'s `min-height` becomes the lesser of what it is now and the cell's own width: the grid is a container (`container: cal-grid / inline-size`), so a cell's width is `calc(100cqw / 7)` and the rule is `min-height: min(5.75rem, calc(100cqw / 7))` (or the equivalent for the rule that gives the phone its 4rem, if there is one: find where 83.2px comes from and make it one rule). A cell with more chips than fit grows, as now (grid rows are auto). At 390 wide the six rows then come to about 336px and the grid is a month, not a column.
- **No room under the Calendar.** The Calendar's column is not typed in (its words are typed in the dialog and the schedule's rows), so it opts out of the caret-middle room the way the Drawing Tool, the Diagram and the Animator do (`src/apps/draw/style.css`: `padding-bottom: var(--to-gutter)` on the column holding the editor, with the comment naming kit.css `.to-main.to-wide`): the same rule in `src/apps/cal/style.css`, with the same comment. The Trip Planner is the same shape (a list edited in rows and a map) and gets the same rule if its column has the room today; measure and say.
- **The placeholder sits where the words will.** In `src/styles/text.css`, `to-text .tx.blank::before` takes the box's padding: `inset: 0; padding: inherit;` (the pseudo-element fills the box and pads as it does, so the placeholder's first letter is where the caret's first letter will be, in every box: the Calendar's What and the schedule's rows, a Spreadsheet cell, a Diagram label, the deck's boxes, Write's page), `pointer-events: none` kept. If a box somewhere relies on the old corner placement (a box whose padding is on a wrapper, not `.tx`, is unaffected), the test below finds it.

## Proof

- `src/apps/cal/tests/editor.test.ts`: at a 390-wide viewport (the test file's way of setting width, or `test/browser/helpers.ts`'s), a day cell's height is at most its width plus one pixel; at 1100 wide the cell is 5.75rem tall; a day with three chips is taller than its neighbour. The column's `padding-bottom` equals `--to-gutter` with the Calendar open (as `test/browser/caret-middle.test.ts` or `scroll.test.ts` checks the Drawing Tool: the same assertion, beside it).
- `test/browser/text.test.ts`, beside the placeholder test: a box with `padding: 13px` on its `.tx`, blank with a placeholder: the placeholder's rect (`getClientRects` of the pseudo-element are not reachable; measure with `getComputedStyle(tx, '::before')`'s `padding-left` and `left`, or compare the placeholder's painted position by `document.caretPositionFromPoint` / the `.tx` first text node's rect after a letter is typed: the first letter's `left` equals what the pseudo-element's content box `left` was) starts at the box's content edge, not its border edge; without the rule it fails by 13px: write it first and watch it fail.
- The Calendar's and the text editor's test files, `npx vitest run` whole, `npm run lint`, `npm run build`; the e2e smoke and a11y specs on the built site (`PW_PORT=4290`), since the phone's a11y run opens the Calendar.

## Not this

- No change to the chips, the agenda, the year or the schedule views beyond the cell rule. No change to the caret-middle room for the columns of words (Write, Outline, Deck, Sheet, Calc). No new padding in the What box: it has its 13px; only the placeholder moves.
