# R19-map-tiles: a map drawn anywhere shows the map, not only the places

**Paul, 2026-10-12**, on a Trip Planner fence on a slide: *"The map shows the locations but not the map itself."* The Trip Planner's page draws the live map with OpenStreetMap's tiles (`src/apps/trip/editor.ts`, Leaflet, `TILES`, the bar's Map switch that turns them off; `src/kit/core/network.ts` `tiles`, allowed as `img`). Its drawn page (`src/apps/trip/view.ts` `tripPage` and `mapSvg`), which a slide, a Word Processor page, an outline item, a preview card and every export use, projects the places with the same Mercator maths onto a plain ground: numbered dots, their names, the lines between. So a map in a deck is a diagram of places with no streets under them.

## The design

- **The drawn map carries the tiles.** `mapSvg` chooses the zoom at which all the places fit the box with a margin (the greatest `z` where the places' extent in tile units fits the box's width and height, between 2 and 18), and lays the tiles of that zoom that cover the box as `<image href="https://tile.openstreetmap.org/{z}/{x}/{y}.png" …>` under the dots and lines, clipped to the box, with `© OpenStreetMap` (a link to `${origin('osm')}/copyright`, as the editor's attribution) in the box's corner in small type. The places' dots and names are drawn as now, over the tiles. One projection for the editor's map, the drawn map and the tiles: the `mx`/`my` helpers, not a second copy.
- **The same switch.** The Trip Planner's Map switch (what `this.tiles` follows: find where it is kept, in the store or the document's head, and use that one place) governs the drawn map too: off, the plain ground as today. A document in a deck or a page draws its map by the same setting, since the person's choice is about telling OpenStreetMap where they look. `src/kit/core/network.ts`'s `tiles` entry says the tiles are asked for by a map drawn anywhere (a slide, a page, an item, a card, an export) as well as the Trip Planner, and `by` names `apps/trip/view.ts` too; `docs/network.md` by its script.
- **Where the picture becomes pixels.** A preview card (`src/kit/share/card.ts`), a PDF, a PNG and a PowerPoint rasterize the SVG (`src/kit/draw/raster.ts`); an `<image>` from another origin taints the canvas unless its bytes are inlined. Before rasterizing a map, the tiles are fetched (`fetch`, `mode: 'cors'`: the host answers with `Access-Control-Allow-Origin: *`; the registry entry gains `connect` beside `img`, so the policy allows it) and inlined as data URLs; a tile that fails to arrive leaves its square the ground's color, never fails the picture. One helper that inlines a drawn map's tiles, used by the raster path, not by the on-screen SVG.
- **OpenStreetMap's tile policy.** At most the tiles the box needs (a slide's box at zoom 14 is a handful; never a whole zoom level), fetched with the browser's own User-Agent, nothing prefetched; the network page says so. Nothing is cached by us beyond the browser's own cache.

## Proof

- `src/apps/trip/tests/view.unit.test.ts` (or where `tripPage` is tested): for three places in Brooklyn in a 320 by 180 box, the chosen zoom is the greatest at which they fit with the margin, the `<image>` tiles cover the box and no more (their count and coordinates), the attribution is present, and every dot lies over a tile; with the switch off, no `<image>` and no attribution. A box with one place centres it at a fixed zoom (say 15).
- `test/browser/trip.test.ts` or the deck's tests: a deck with a `trip` fence draws tile images on its slide (their `href` on the OpenStreetMap host; in the test the host is unreachable and the image's load failing is fine) and the plain ground when the Map switch is off.
- `test/browser/share.test.ts` or `card`'s tests: a card made of a trip with the tiles stubbed (the fetch stub answers a 1 by 1 PNG) rasterizes without throwing and the PNG is not blank where a tile is; with the fetch failing, the card still comes (the ground where the tile was).
- `scripts/check-build.ts` and `test/csp.test.ts` with the `connect` source; `npm run build`; the smoke, keyboard and a11y specs; `e2e/cards.spec.ts --project=desktop` (kill any stray `workerd` first: `pkill -x workerd`, and free ports 8797 and 8798 with `fuser -k`; the spec starts its own).

## Not this

- No tiles in the editor's thumbnails on the home page (templates' previews stay the plain ground: a visitor who has not opened a trip tells OpenStreetMap nothing); say in the report how the preview path is told apart (the `thumb` option, if `view` gets it). No other tile host, no API key, no caching of tiles in the service worker.
